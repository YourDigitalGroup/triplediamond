Appraisals & Insurance

GIA Appraisals & Jewelry Insurance

Know exactly what your jewelry is worth — and make sure it’s protected. We offer GIA written appraisals, the industry-recognized standard for assessing diamonds, gemstones, and fine jewelry, along with jewelry insurance options, all in one visit. Our gemologist is GIA trained at the New York campus and holds over twelve gemology certifications, including GIAs prestigious Ethics and Law.

GIA Appraisals

Our GIA-trained jeweler personally evaluates and documents:

  • Diamonds (cut, clarity, color, carat)
  • Colored gemstones
  • Fine jewelry and estate pieces
  • Custom and one-of-a-kind pieces

A GIA appraisal gives you a documented, credible valuation — useful for insurance, resale, estate planning, or simple peace of mind.

Jewelry Insurance

Once your piece is appraised, we can help you set up jewelry insurance so it's protected against loss, theft, or damage — no separate errand required. Ask our staff for current details when you bring your piece in.
