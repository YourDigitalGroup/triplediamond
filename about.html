About Us · Ocean Springs, Since 2016

Over 100 Years of Combined Experience, One Local Shop

Triple Diamond opened in Ocean Springs in 2016 with a simple idea: pawn shops and jewelry stores don’t have to feel like two different worlds. We built a shop where a first-time pawn customer and a bride shopping for a diamond feel equally at home.

Our Story

Fair Value, Explained Clearly

Our founders combined decades of hands-on pawn brokering with real jewelry-trade expertise to build Triple Diamond. Our team — including a GIA-trained jeweler with hands-on bench experience — brings together more than 100 years of combined experience across pawn brokering, fine jewelry, gemology, and watch expertise.

That depth means every item that crosses our counter — from a gold chain to a semi-automatic rifle to a loose diamond — gets evaluated by someone who actually knows what they're looking at.
